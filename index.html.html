About me

I'm a finance student pursuing a dual degree in B.Com and BBA (DBE), and I'm currently preparing for the CFA Level 1 exam. I'm interested in how businesses use numbers to make decisions.

In my coursework I've built DCF valuation and three-statement financial models in Excel. Building them showed me how a single assumption flows through a company's financial statements and changes its value. I rely on analytical thinking and problem solving to work through each model step by step.

Outside the classroom, I've helped in my family's wires and cables business and brought in a few new clients. It gave me a first-hand view of how a business finds and works with its customers.

I'm now looking for my first role in finance, where I can apply my modelling skills, keep learning and grow.
